This guide contains the exact, high-scoring answers to all TA Viva questions for **Engineering Lab 06 (ShopKart: Checkout & Orders — Payment Integration, Snapshot Design & Server Revalidation)**.

## 📌 Top 21 Mandatory TA Viva Questions & Word-for-Word Answers

---

### Q1: Why must the Razorpay Order be created from the backend?
> **Answer:**
> - **Financial Integrity & Security:** Creating an order requires the `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET`. The backend connects to Razorpay to establish the trusted transaction amount.
> - If orders were created from the client, a malicious user could inspect the network request, alter the amount (e.g. change ₹7,497 to ₹1), and pay a fraction of the cost. The backend owns and computes the authoritative total.

---

### Q2: Why is the Razorpay Key Secret never sent to React or frontend clients?
> **Answer:**
> - The `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET` grants unrestricted administrative access to your Razorpay merchant account (initiating refunds, transferring funds, viewing customer transactions).
> - Any code or environment variable in React is completely visible in the browser (via DevTools, Network tab, or JavaScript bundles).
> - Only `RAZORPAY_KEY_ID` (the public identifier) is shared with the client. The Key Secret remains strictly on the backend.

---

### Q3: Why does Razorpay expect INR amounts in paise (e.g., ₹7,497 as 749700)?
> **Answer:**
> - Financial systems avoid floating-point math issues ($0.1 + 0.2 \neq 0.3$) by storing currency in its smallest sub-unit.
> - For Indian Rupees (INR), $1\text{ Rupee} = 100\text{ paise}$. Therefore, $\text{amountInPaise} = \text{Math.round}(\text{totalAmount} \times 100)$.

---

### Q4: What are `razorpay_order_id`, `razorpay_payment_id`, and `razorpay_signature`?
> **Answer:**
> - **`razorpay_order_id`**: The unique order identifier generated by Razorpay's backend when the order was initiated.
> - **`razorpay_payment_id`**: The unique transaction identifier generated by Razorpay when the customer completes payment.
> - **`razorpay_signature`**: An HMAC-SHA256 hash created by Razorpay using `razorpay_order_id + "|" + razorpay_payment_id` encrypted with your secret key. It is cryptographic proof that the payment actually succeeded on Razorpay's servers without client tampering.

---

### Q5: Why is payment signature verification mandatory on the backend?
> **Answer:**
> - The browser environment is untrusted. Anyone could use Postman or curl to call `/orders/verify-payment` with a fake payload pretending that a payment succeeded.
> - Verifying the HMAC-SHA256 signature on the server using `crypto.createHmac('sha256', process.env.RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET)` guarantees that only legitimate payments processed by Razorpay are confirmed.

---

### Q6: What would happen if the server simply accepted `{ paymentSuccess: true }` from the client?
> **Answer:**
> - Any user could open Developer Tools, execute a fetch request with `{ paymentSuccess: true }`, and get products delivered for free without paying a single rupee.

---

### Q7: Why must the cart NOT be cleared when Checkout merely opens or when the Razorpay modal appears?
> **Answer:**
> - The customer might close the Razorpay modal, experience a network drop, or have their bank decline the card.
> - Clearing the cart prematurely would cause the customer to lose all their selected items upon payment failure.
> - The cart is cleared **only after the payment signature is verified** on the backend (`POST /orders/verify-payment`).

---

### Q8: Why does an Order store product name, price, and image snapshot separately instead of only referencing the Product ID?
> **Answer:**
> - **Historical Immutability:** An order represents a legal, financial contract of what was purchased at that exact moment in time.
> - If an admin later edits the keyboard price from ₹2,999 to ₹3,499, or changes its name, or deletes the product entirely, an order referencing only the Product ID would show altered prices or crash.
> - By snapshotting `{ name, price, quantity, image }` inside `items`, the order remains permanent and legally accurate forever.

---

### Q9: What is the difference between Cart data and Order data?
> **Answer:**
> | Feature | **Cart Data** | **Order Data** |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Nature** | Transient, mutable intent. | Permanent, immutable historical business record. |
> | **Storage** | Dynamic references (`ObjectId` to `Product`). | Independent snapshots of name, price, quantity. |
> | **Lifecycle** | Can be modified, cleared, or replaced anytime. | Persisted forever once payment is confirmed. |

---

### Q10: Why must the backend calculate `totalAmount` instead of trusting the frontend?
> **Answer:**
> - If the frontend sent `{ totalAmount: 1 }` or calculated the total in React, a user could manipulate the JavaScript variable or network payload to buy a ₹10,000 item for ₹1.
> - The backend re-queries the `Product` collection, fetches the true unit prices, multiplies by cart quantities, and derives `totalAmount` authoritatively.

---

### Q11: Why must stock be re-verified during Checkout (`POST /orders/create-payment-order`)?
> **Answer:**
> - A customer might have added 3 keyboards to their cart hours ago when stock was 10.
> - Meanwhile, other shoppers purchased the keyboards, reducing stock to 1.
> - Re-checking `if (item.quantity > product.stock)` ensures the shop does not accept payment for inventory that cannot be fulfilled.

---

### Q12: How do you prevent a user from viewing another user's order (IDOR Protection)?
> **Answer:**
> - Every order stores a `user: { type: ObjectId, ref: 'Customer' }` field.
> - In `GET /orders/:id`, we compare:
>   ```javascript
>   if (order.user.toString() !== req.user._id.toString()) {
>     return res.status(403).json({ success: false, message: 'Access denied' });
>   }
>   ```
> - In `GET /orders`, we query only `Order.find({ user: req.user._id })`. Users cannot access orders belonging to other accounts.

---

### Q13: What happens to product stock after successful payment verification?
> **Answer:**
> - In `verifyPayment` controller, for each item in `order.items`, we decrement inventory:
>   ```javascript
>   await Product.findByIdAndUpdate(item.product, { $inc: { stock: -item.quantity } });
>   ```
> - This keeps physical stock accurately reflected for subsequent shoppers.

---

### Q14: Why must global frontend Cart state be updated after successful payment?
> **Answer:**
> - While the database cart is cleared in MongoDB, the React state in `CartContext` remains in browser memory.
> - If we don't call `refreshCart()`, the Navbar will still show `Cart (3)`, confusing the user into thinking their cart wasn't cleared. Calling `refreshCart()` synchronizes the UI with the cleared database state.

---

### Q15: What happens if a user navigates directly to `/checkout` with an empty cart?
> **Answer:**
> - On the frontend, `Checkout.jsx` checks `cartItems.length === 0` and renders an empty state with a "Browse Products" link, preventing form submission.
> - On the backend, `POST /orders/create-payment-order` checks `customer.cart.length === 0` and rejects the request with `400 Bad Request`.

---

### Q16: How does Shipping Form validation work?
> **Answer:**
> - **Client-side:** Validates that Full Name, Address, City, State are non-empty after trimming whitespace; Phone has at least 10 digits; Pincode is exactly 6 digits (`/^\d{6}$/`).
> - **Server-side:** Strictly validates all 6 fields in `createPaymentOrder`, rejecting empty whitespace or malformed inputs with `400 Bad Request`.

---

### Q17: What HTTP methods and status codes are used for Order REST APIs?
> **Answer:**
> | Action | Endpoint | Method | Success Status | Error Status |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | Create Payment Order | `/orders/create-payment-order` | `POST` | `200 OK` | `400 Bad Request`, `401 Unauthorized` |
> | Verify Payment | `/orders/verify-payment` | `POST` | `200 OK` | `400 Bad Request`, `403 Forbidden` |
> | Get All My Orders | `/orders` | `GET` | `200 OK` | `401 Unauthorized` |
> | Get Single Order | `/orders/:id` | `GET` | `200 OK` | `404 Not Found`, `403 Forbidden` |
> | Update Status (Bonus) | `/orders/:id/status` | `PATCH` | `200 OK` | `400 Bad Request` |

---

### Q18: What is the lifecycle of order status from creation to delivery?
> **Answer:**
> 1. `PENDING_PAYMENT` (ShopKart order created, waiting for Razorpay checkout)
> 2. `PLACED` (Signature verified, payment completed, stock decremented, cart cleared)
> 3. `CONFIRMED` (Merchant accepted order)
> 4. `SHIPPED` (Dispatched for delivery)
> 5. `DELIVERED` (Successfully handed over to customer)

---

### Q19: What loading states are implemented during Checkout?
> **Answer:**
> 1. Button loading spinner with text *"Processing Order & Payment..."* and disabled state to prevent duplicate submissions.
> 2. Razorpay checkout modal loading.
> 3. Verification handshake with backend.

---

### Q20: How are previous orders displayed on the "My Orders" page?
> **Answer:**
> - Sorted newest first (`createdAt: -1`).
> - Displays Order ID, formatted date (e.g., `12 Oct 2026`), status badge, item list with snapshot images and quantities, and total amount.
> - Supports full Loading, Error, and Empty states (*"You have not placed any orders yet. [Start Shopping]"*).

---

### Q21: What happens if Razorpay Checkout fails or is cancelled?
> **Answer:**
> - `paymentObject.on('payment.failed', ...)` and `modal.ondismiss` callbacks capture the event.
> - The UI displays: *"Payment was cancelled. Your cart has been preserved."*
> - The cart remains 100% intact so the user can retry without losing their items.
